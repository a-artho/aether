Aether 1.0.6 fixes automatic tunnel recovery after unlocking or returning from the background. Delayed lifecycle events and older connection checks can no longer overwrite newer connection attempts.

Recovery now waits for connection events instead of polling, reuses freshly verified stable connections, and avoids redundant connection-state UI updates. Automatic connection preferences and active installation/spoofing behavior are preserved.

The IPA is fully unsigned and includes the tunnel extension. Sign it with your own certificate before installing.
